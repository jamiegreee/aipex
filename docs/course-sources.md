# AIPEX course sources

Reviewed 13 October 2026. All 24 lessons include explicit source connections and at least two distinct annotated external readings. This supplements the original AIPEX fictional cases and exercises; it does not change lesson slugs or completion requirements.

Sources span UK government guidance, ICO regulator guidance, NIST, OECD, DRCF, independent Ada Lovelace Institute / AI Now / OGP research and the Model Cards / Datasheets papers. Source inclusion is not an endorsement. The Green Book and Magenta Book links point to the 2026 editions. The procurement URL is corrected. The AI Playbook’s older legal wording is explicitly distinguished from current ICO guidance.

## AI Policy in Practice

### A policy problem, not a technology

- [HM Treasury: The Green Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2/the-green-book-2026): Read the rationale and objectives discussion. Rewrite Northbridge's assignment without assuming an AI solution.

- [Government Digital Service: Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard): Read points 1 and 2 on user needs and whole problems. Identify an applicant need missing from the supplier's framing.

### Objectives and a theory of change

- [HM Treasury: The Magenta Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html): Read section 2.2.1, Understanding the intervention. Mark a weak causal link and an assumption in your Northbridge theory of change.

- [OECD: AI Principles](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/ai-principles.html): Read the values-based principles. Choose a relevant outcome and explain how you would observe it; these principles are not a substitute for domestic law.

### Audit an evidence claim

- [Mitchell et al.: Model Cards for Model Reporting (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993): Read the model-card reporting framework, especially intended use and evaluation. List three disclosures missing from Northbridge's supplier claim.

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Read MEASURE 2.1, 2.3 and 2.5. Ask whether the test population and conditions support the proposed recruitment use.

### Research under uncertainty

- [HM Treasury: The Magenta Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html): Read the discussion of evaluation purpose and questions in section 2.2.2. Rank Northbridge's uncertainties by whether resolving them could change the recommendation.

- [DSIT: Introduction to AI assurance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-to-ai-assurance/introduction-to-ai-assurance): Read AI assurance mechanisms in section 4.2. Choose an evidence-gathering technique and explain why its result would be useful within six weeks.

### Institutions and authority

- [Digital Regulation Cooperation Forum: roles and remit](https://www.drcf.org.uk/about-us): Read Who we are, Members and How we work. Separate each member regulator's role from the forum's coordination function; follow remit links before making a real legal claim.

- [OECD: AI Principles](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/ai-principles.html): Read Accountability. Assign a responsible actor to each Northbridge decision, then identify the separate evidence needed to establish that actor's authority.

### Stakeholders and legitimacy

- [Cabinet Office: Consultation Principles](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/consultation-principles-guidance/consultation-principles-2018): Read principles 2, 4, 6 and 7. Design an accessible way to hear from applicants before Northbridge has settled on an option.

- [Ada Lovelace Institute, AI Now and OGP: Algorithmic accountability for the public sector (2021)](https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/report/algorithmic-accountability-public-sector/): Read the report's policy examples and lessons. Choose an accountability mechanism and explain what would need adapting to Northbridge; this is historical comparative research, not a map of current law.

### Build a real options set

- [HM Treasury: The Green Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2/the-green-book-2026): Read the longlist and shortlist appraisal material. Construct alternatives that differ in how they address the barrier to review, including a credible non-AI option.

- [Ada Lovelace Institute, AI Now and OGP: Algorithmic accountability for the public sector (2021)](https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/report/algorithmic-accountability-public-sector/): Compare two accountability-policy examples in the report. Identify their intended mechanism and one condition that could prevent it working in Northbridge.

### Appraisal and distribution

- [HM Treasury: The Green Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2/the-green-book-2026): Read chapter 7 on distributional analysis. Describe which applicants, employers and service teams bear the costs and benefits of each option.

- [UK Government: Data and AI Ethics Framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/data-ethics-framework/data-and-ai-ethics-framework): Read Fairness and Trade-offs. Identify an impact that an average performance measure would conceal, and propose evidence to investigate it.

### Delivery and accountability

- [UK Government: AI Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-playbook-for-the-uk-government/artificial-intelligence-playbook-for-the-uk-government-html): Read the material on AI governance and organisational capability. Identify a staffing or implementation dependency in Northbridge's recommendation.

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Read GOVERN, focusing on responsibilities and risk-management processes. Assign owners for review, escalation and changes to the service.

### Evaluation and adaptation

- [HM Treasury: The Magenta Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html): Read the evaluation-design and interpretation material. Propose an outcome measure, a comparator and a limitation on the conclusion you could draw.

- [Government Digital Service: Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard): Read point 10 on defining success and publishing performance data. Decide which Northbridge measures should be shared and what context they need.

### Write a decision brief

- [HM Treasury: The Green Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2/the-green-book-2026): Read the balanced judgement on value for money and presentation of appraisal results. Identify a material trade-off your briefing must disclose.

- [DSIT: Introduction to AI assurance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-to-ai-assurance/introduction-to-ai-assurance): Read the Communicate activity and assurance mechanisms. Separate established evidence, proposed tests and residual uncertainty in your brief.

### Capstone: your policy recommendation

- [HM Treasury: The Green Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2/the-green-book-2026): Use the objectives, options and appraisal material to check the recommendation against its strongest alternative.

- [HM Treasury: The Magenta Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html): Use the theory-of-change and evaluation material to test your delivery and learning plan.

- [Ada Lovelace Institute, AI Now and OGP: Algorithmic accountability for the public sector (2021)](https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/report/algorithmic-accountability-public-sector/): Use one comparative example to challenge a proposed accountability mechanism. Explain its relevance and a limit on transfer to Northbridge.

## Governing AI in Public Services

### Start with the service

- [Government Digital Service: Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard): Read points 1, 2 and 3. Add a resident journey or contact channel missing from your service map.

- [UK Government: AI Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-playbook-for-the-uk-government/artificial-intelligence-playbook-for-the-uk-government-html): Read the guidance on choosing an AI use case and assessing suitability. Identify a credible improvement Eastmere could make without this tool.

### Purpose and system boundaries

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Read MAP 1 and 2. List users, affected residents, permitted uses and a context change that would require reassessment.

- [Mitchell et al.: Model Cards for Model Reporting (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993): Read intended uses and caveats in the proposed model-card framework. Draft a limitation that staff could act on in the housing-enquiry workflow.

### Risk and affected people

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Read MAP 5 on impacts. Trace one omitted housing-enquiry detail through the workflow to a possible resident harm.

- [UK Government: Data and AI Ethics Framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/data-ethics-framework/data-and-ai-ethics-framework): Read Fairness and Societal impact. Identify an affected group and specify what evidence or participation is missing from the assessment.

### Evaluate supplier evidence

- [Mitchell et al.: Model Cards for Model Reporting (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993): Read the reporting framework's evaluation material. Request results relevant to housing enquiries, including limitations and differences between groups.

- [Gebru et al.: Datasheets for Datasets (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010): Read the questions on dataset composition, collection and recommended uses. Identify a data mismatch that could undermine the supplier's result.

### Procurement and contracts

- [UK Government: Digital, Data and Technology Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook-html): Read the testing, contract-management and exit-planning material. Specify an evidence obligation and an exit requirement for Eastmere's contract.

- [DSIT: Introduction to AI assurance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-to-ai-assurance/introduction-to-ai-assurance): Read section 4.2 on assurance mechanisms. Choose how a supplier claim should be tested and identify who can examine the result.

### Data and access

- [Information Commissioner's Office: Data Protection Impact Assessments](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/): Read what a DPIA assesses and how to carry one out. Map a resident-data flow and a question for Eastmere's data-protection lead; check current ICO update notices and applicable law.

- [Gebru et al.: Datasheets for Datasets (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010): Read collection, processing, distribution and maintenance questions. Record retention, access and a change that would need review.

### Human review that works

- [UK Government: AI Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-playbook-for-the-uk-government/artificial-intelligence-playbook-for-the-uk-government-html): Read the operational Human oversight discussion. Design a review step that can detect an omitted urgent detail. Use current ICO guidance for legal requirements rather than relying on this playbook's older legal wording.

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Read GOVERN 2 and 3 on responsibilities and workforce practices. Specify training, escalation and the authority to override or pause a suggestion.

### Transparency and challenge

- [Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard: hub and scope](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/algorithmic-transparency-recording-standard-hub): Use the hub's recording guidance and scope policy. Draft a plain-language tool description and check whether the standard is mandatory or voluntary for the organisation in a real case.

- [Ada Lovelace Institute, AI Now and OGP: Algorithmic accountability for the public sector (2021)](https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/report/algorithmic-accountability-public-sector/): Compare transparency and accountability-policy examples. Explain what a resident could do with Eastmere's disclosure and what challenge route is still needed.

### Pilot and launch gates

- [HM Treasury: The Magenta Book (2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html): Read evaluation design and the purpose of evaluation questions. Specify the comparator, outcomes and limits on what Eastmere's pilot can establish.

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Read MEASURE 2 and MANAGE 1. Define a launch condition, an unacceptable failure and the decision owner for each.

### Monitor, handle incidents and change

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Read MANAGE and MEASURE 3 on tracking risks. Identify a signal, escalation route and reassessment trigger for Eastmere.

- [UK Government: Digital, Data and Technology Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook-html): Read continuity and exit planning. Describe how Eastmere would maintain service, recover information and switch to its fallback if the supplier failed.

### An assurance pack people can use

- [DSIT: Introduction to AI assurance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-to-ai-assurance/introduction-to-ai-assurance): Read assurance mechanisms and key actions for organisations. Link each proposed assurance claim to evidence, an assessor and a limitation.

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Review GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE. Find a missing part of Eastmere's pack; the framework is a risk-management tool, not an approval certificate.

### Capstone: your deployment decision

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework: Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/): Use the four core functions to check the evidence and conditions behind Eastmere's launch, revise or stop decision.

- [Ada Lovelace Institute, AI Now and OGP: Algorithmic accountability for the public sector (2021)](https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/report/algorithmic-accountability-public-sector/): Use one report example to challenge whether the proposed disclosure and review process would give residents meaningful influence.

- [Information Commissioner's Office: Data Protection Impact Assessments](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/): Use the assessment questions to identify a data-protection issue requiring specialist review before a real deployment. Check the latest regulator guidance.
