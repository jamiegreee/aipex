# International case-library expansion

Reviewed 18 October 2026. Nine documented cases across seven countries, including the three existing UK cases. This is a selected teaching collection, not a worldwide inventory or a representative survey of success and failure. Country is the primary institutional/study setting; it is not every country represented in a research dataset or applicant population.

## Learning design

Every new case has a dated decision context, reported evidence with section/paragraph locators, explicit evidence limits, three concrete tasks, a disclosed AIPEX worked approach and a tool launch that seeds a separate draft. The cases span institutional design, deployment oversight and evidence appraisal. They include published operating approaches and diagnostic research as well as enforcement/inquiry cases.

Proposed controls, options and evaluation designs are analysis, not reported deployments or commitments by the institutions concerned. No local costs, approvals, outcomes or owners are invented to complete a case.

## Browsing and exports

Country and topic filters combine with text search across case titles, settings and evidence. England’s case belongs under the United Kingdom country filter; China/India applicants do not turn the Canadian institutional case into a Chinese or Indian case. A clear-filter action remains available when filters return results. The homepage highlights three international examples instead of growing into a nine-card catalogue.

Case-derived Markdown exports retain the primary source URL, relevant source sections and scope notes. Tests cover combined filters, unique slugs, country grouping, all case-to-tool field references and source provenance in each new case’s export. Existing private/account storage is unchanged.
